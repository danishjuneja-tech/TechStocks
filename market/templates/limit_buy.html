💡 How Limit Buy Works

Set a price below the current price.

Example:
Current price = ₹100
Limit buy = ₹90

Your order stays pending until the stock reaches ₹90 or lower.

Then the system automatically buys the shares at the current market price.
⚠️ Do not place the same limit buy repeatedly.
Once placed, check your Portfolio for the pending order and cancel it there if needed.
